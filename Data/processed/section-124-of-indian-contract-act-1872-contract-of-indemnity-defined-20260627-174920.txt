The Indian Contract Act, 1872
Chapter-VIII Of Indemnity and Guarantee
Section 124: “Contract of indemnity” defined.
124. A contract by which one party promises to save the other from loss caused
to him by the conduct of the promisor himself, or by the conduct of any other
person, is called a “contract of indemnity”.
Illustration
A contracts to indemnify B against the consequences of any proceedings
which C may take against B in respect of a certain sum of 200 rupees. This
is a contract of indemnity.
